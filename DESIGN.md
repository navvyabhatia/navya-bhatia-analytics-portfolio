# Navya Bhatia Portfolio Design Direction

This portfolio adapts the light, geometric enterprise language documented in
VoltAgent's `awesome-design-md` HP analysis for a personal analytics brand.

## Visual thesis

An editorial decision brief: white-paper clarity, electric-blue signals, a
single coral highlight, strong geometry, and data-shaped details. The site
should feel polished and human-made rather than like a generic portfolio
template.

## Tokens

- Canvas: `#ffffff`
- Cloud surface: `#f5f7fb`
- Ink: `#111318`
- Muted ink: `#5e6472`
- Electric blue: `#075ee8`
- Deep blue: `#0b2b59`
- Coral signal: `#ff5b55`
- Hairline: `#dfe4ec`
- Corners: 12-24px on major cards, 4px on utility controls
- Typography: system sans with tight display tracking and generous body leading

## Interaction

- Recruiter actions remain visible in the first viewport.
- Motion is brief and functional, with a reduced-motion fallback.
- Hover states use color, translation, and border changes without heavy shadows.
